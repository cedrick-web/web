## The DevSprint method

Every major challenge trains the same repeatable process:

**Understand → Identify Input/Output → Break Down → Choose a Technique → Design the Algorithm → Write Pseudocode → Implement → Test → Debug → Analyze Complexity → Improve**

The goal is not to memorize 60 answers. The goal is to become better at producing your own answers.
